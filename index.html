About the Workshop

Modern machine learning has been driven by training ever-larger models on ever-larger datasets, yet our scientific understanding of training itself lags behind. This workshop brings together researchers studying the science of training: the principles, laws, and phenomena that govern how models learn at scale.

We invite work spanning empirical phenomena, theoretical frameworks, and methodological contributions that advance our understanding of training dynamics — from small models to frontier-scale systems.

Topics of interest

  • Scaling laws and predictability
  • Optimization dynamics and loss landscapes
  • Data curation, mixing, and curriculum
  • Emergent capabilities and phase transitions
  • Mechanistic interpretability of training
  • Pretraining vs. post-training trade-offs
  • Compute-optimal training and efficiency
  • Reproducibility and training instabilities

Call for Papers

We welcome submissions of original work, position papers, and experience reports. Papers should be up to 8 pages (excluding references and appendix) in the workshop template.
